Kids Studio

A private production tool, operated by Muslimin.

Kids Studio produces short, gentle children's story videos (for example traditional Indonesian folktales with a positive lesson) and uploads them to YouTube channels that its owner runs.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts and nobody else signs in. The only Google account it is connected to is the owner's own, and it uses that access for one purpose: uploading the owner's videos, with their captions, to the owner's channels.

  • Videos are uploaded as private (or scheduled), marked "made for kids" where the channel is for children.
  • No viewer data, comments or analytics of other people are collected.
