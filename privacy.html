Privacy policy

Kids Studio · effective 19 November 2026 · updated 19 November 2026: reads the statistics of the owner's own videos

Who runs Kids Studio

Kids Studio is a private tool run by Muslimin (contact: MusliminDH@gmail.com). It has no public users: the only person who signs in with Google is its owner.

Google data it accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Kids Studio asks for these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel;
  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's name and id, its playlists, and the status of videos it uploaded;
  • youtube.force-ssl: to add captions to those videos and to add them to the owner's playlists;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the statistics YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel and videos (views, watch time, average percentage watched, audience retention, shares, likes and subscribers gained).

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's own videos, with their titles, descriptions, captions and playlist placement, to the owner's own channels, and to show their upload status to the owner.

The statistics are read once a day, only for the owner's own channel and videos, and only to show them to the owner and to suggest which topics, story openings, video lengths and upload times to use next. Every suggestion waits for the owner's approval. These statistics are aggregate totals as YouTube reports them: Kids Studio does not read or collect data about individual viewers, their comments or their accounts, nor other people's content, and it does not use Google data for advertising.

Storage and security

The OAuth tokens are stored encrypted, and the statistics are stored, on the owner's private server, which is not exposed to the internet. They are never sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else, and are not used to train any model.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while a channel is connected. Disconnecting the channel in Kids Studio deletes its token, which also stops any further reading of statistics. Statistics already read are kept for the owner's own reference and are deleted on the owner's request. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Kids Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by YouTube: policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new effective date.
